## First Steps: Understanding Core Weir AI Concepts

To effectively use our API, it's important to understand a few fundamental concepts:

### 🏢 Organizations

* An "Organization" represents your company, business, or entity within the Weir AI system.
* It's the primary container for all your data, users, and configurations on our platform.
* Organizations can be self-onboarded via the API.

### 👤 Users

* "Users" are individual accounts associated with an Organization.
* Users have specific roles and permissions.
* The first user to register and create an Organization typically becomes its initial administrator.
